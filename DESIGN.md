## Brand & Style

This design system blends the rigor of modern architectural design with the grounded warmth of natural earth minerals. Designed for high-clarity healthcare applications, clinical translation engines, and patient adherence platforms, the visual tone departs from sterile clinical blues and cold monochromatic interfaces. Instead, it pairs rich terracotta, luminous amber ochre, lively forest olive, and deep smoked charcoal over warm limestone surfaces.

### Emotional Demeanor
- **Reassuring & Grounded:** Earth-derived mineral tones replace anxiety-inducing clinical palettes with warmth, stability, and human connection.
- **Rationalist Precision:** Crisp hairline structures, clear visual hierarchies, and tight typographical metrics instill trust and operational efficiency.
- **Cognitive Ease:** Color explicitly signals semantic meaning—clinical AI translations pop in warm ochre, routine adherence rests in calm olive, and definitive actions assert themselves in terracotta.

### Design Movement
- **Modern Architectural Rationalism:** Inspired by structural balance, clean grid discipline, tactile mineral surfaces, subtle hairline contours, and accessible data ergonomics.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from mineral pigments, calibrated for contrast and functional clarity in healthcare contexts.

### Functional Palette Roles

- **Primary Action (Mineral Terracotta / Warm Rust - `#B34720`):** Used for primary conversion points, critical interactive states, schedule action buttons, and active selection milestones.
  - Hover: `#9B3B18`
  - Active: `#7E2F11`
  - Surface Tint: `#F7ECE8`
- **Structural Header & Chrome (Smoked Charcoal Slate - `#1E232E`):** Anchors navigation panels, top headers, dense data headings, and high-emphasis body text. Provides a structural frame against limestone backgrounds.
  - Secondary Slate Text: `#454D5D`
  - Subtle Muted Slate: `#727A8A`
- **AI Simplification & Insights (Luminous Amber Ochre - `#D98A1B`):** Dedicated exclusively to synthesized AI output, plain-language patient translation badges, diagnostic alerts, and clinical key takeaway highlights.
  - Accent / Tag Background: `#FCF4E7`
  - Deep Ochre Border: `#B8710F`
- **Adherence & Regimen Status (Lively Forest Olive Earth - `#527A40` / `#436B36`):** Applied to daily medication completion checkmarks, dosing streaks, on-track metric bars, and confirmed historical records.
  - Background Tint: `#EFF5EC`
  - Deep Olive Border: `#3B5E2F`
- **Supporting Mineral Neutral (Warm Sandstone / Rich Beige - `#C9B39F`):** Used for inactive track bars, neutral metric containers, and dosage scale indicators.
- **Canvas Base (Warm Limestone / Soft Oatmeal - `#FBF9F5`):** The persistent backdrop for the overall interface, reducing eye strain and softening high-contrast elements.
- **Card Surfaces & Outlines:** Primary elevated card surfaces are pure `#FFFFFF` with precise, hairline structural mineral borders in `#E5E0D8`.

## Typography

The typography uses Plus Jakarta Sans throughout for clean, geometric rationality paired with human warmth. Its generous x-height and open counters guarantee legibility across lab charts, micro-dosages, and long-form medical explanations.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Section Headers & Module Titles:** Set in uppercase or heavy weights using `#1E232E` with negative letter-spacing for structural authority.
- **Medical Text & Summaries:** Set in standard body sizing (`14px` or `16px`) with relaxed line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to prevent visual crowding when processing complex clinical terminology.
- **Labels & Status Pills:** Styled with medium or small label tokens, tight letter-spacing, and clear weight differentiation (`600` or `700`) to guarantee legibility at glance speed.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point spatial rhythm with strict geometric compartmentalization. Modules align cleanly along an architectural grid, avoiding floating or disjointed elements.

### Breakpoints & Grid Composition
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout. Margin of `2rem` (32px), gutter of `1.5rem` (24px). Persistent dark slate navigation rail (`240px` width) pinned to the left, with the main content area expanding within a maximum container width of `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout. Margin of `1.5rem` (24px), gutter of `1rem` (16px). Navigation collapses to an icon rail (`72px`) or sticky mineral top bar.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Margin of `1rem` (16px), gutter of `1rem` (16px). Cards stack vertically into single-column flows, with sticky bottom action anchors for primary actions.

### Internal Spacing Rhythm
- **Component Padding:** Standard cards use `1.5rem` internal padding for desktop and `1rem` on mobile.
- **Data Gap Rhythm:** Tight medical data pairs use `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px). Structural section dividers use `space-lg` (24px) to `space-xl` (32px).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids blurred dramatic drop-shadows, using instead structural planar layering inspired by architectural drawings and stacked mineral slabs.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Base Foundation (Level 0):** `#FBF9F5` (Warm Limestone). The non-interactive background.
2. **Elevated Card Slabs (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White). Cards sit flush on the background, delineated by a razor-thin border (`1px solid #E5E0D8`) and supported by an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(30, 35, 46, 0.03), 0 4px 12px rgba(30, 35, 46, 0.02)`.
3. **Floating Overlays & Popovers (Level 2):** Modals, flyouts, and dropdowns retain the white slab surface with a firmer border (`1px solid #D5CEC4`) and a warm ambient grounding shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(30, 35, 46, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(30, 35, 46, 0.04)`.
4. **Permanent Structural Chrome:** Navigation rails and app chrome use solid `#1E232E` with no drop shadow, grounding the interface through pure value contrast.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp architectural lines with soft, accessible handling:
- **Base Geometry (`roundedness: 2`):** Standard interactive elements, input fields, cards, and modal dialogs use an exact radius of `8px` (`0.5rem`). This strikes a balance between professional precision and approachable touch targets.
- **Nested Inner Containers:** Sub-panels within cards (such as lab report previews or AI translation blocks) use a reduced radius of `6px` to maintain concentric alignment.
- **Pills & Status Indicators:** Status badges, adherence pill tags, and AI summary tags use full pill rounding (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish metadata from structural containers.
